Covalence connects your iPhone and your Apple Account to elementary OS. There is nothing to install on the iPhone: everything goes through the computer's Bluetooth and Apple's online services.

## Two independent connections
- **Your iPhone, over Bluetooth**, when it is near the computer: notifications, music controls, messages, contacts, call history, calls, battery.
- **Your Apple Account, over the Internet**: iCloud mail, calendars, reminders and contacts, and if you wish iCloud Drive and iCloud Photos.

Use one, the other or both. The [first-run setup](app:setup) walks you through it step by step.

## The Covalence apps
- **Covalence** gathers everything in a sidebar: Overview, Messages, Phone, Contacts, Notifications, Headphones, Apple Services, Settings.
- **Messages**, **Phone**, **Contacts** and **Headphones** also exist as separate apps, with their own window and dock icon. Choose which ones to show in [Settings](app:settings).

!tip Press F1 in any Covalence window to open this guide again. The small "?" buttons open the right page directly.

## Good to know
- Covalence is not affiliated with or endorsed by Apple. It uses what the iPhone agrees to share with a Bluetooth accessory, which has limits: no attachments and no replies in group conversations, for example.
- Everything stays on your computer. Covalence has no server and collects nothing: see [Privacy](guide:privacy).

!tip The English version of Covalence is in beta. Some screens may still show French text.
