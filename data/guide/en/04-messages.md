!warn **Show Notifications** must be on, on the iPhone (Settings › Bluetooth › ⓘ). See [Connect your iPhone](guide:link).

## What you see
- The iPhone only shares its recent messages. The history builds up in Covalence day after day and stays on this computer.
- Until a message is read on the iPhone, Covalence only shows its beginning (about 120 characters), so that it does not mark it as read for you. The full text arrives once the message is read.
- iMessages and group conversations may be incomplete: the iPhone only shares part of them. Photos, videos and attachments are not shared.

## Reply
1. Open the conversation.
2. Type your text at the bottom, then press Enter. Shift+Enter starts a new line.
3. A thin bar under the header shows the sending. The message leaves from your iPhone, with your number.

- Sending only works in conversations with **one person**. Group conversations can be read here but are answered on the iPhone.
- A message that could not be sent stays in the conversation with **Retry** and **Delete**. Covalence never resends anything by itself, to avoid duplicates.
- **New message**, at the top of the list: pick a contact or type a number.
- In a notification, **Reply** opens a small quick-reply window.

## Reactions
- When someone reacts to a message (❤️, 👍, 😂…), the reaction shows as a small pill on that bubble, with who reacted when you hover it. A removed reaction disappears.
- To react: right-click a bubble, then **React** and an emoji, or **Other emoji…**. Only in conversations with one person.

!warn Bluetooth only carries text. Reactions you receive arrive as SMS (“Loved “see you””), which Covalence recognises in French and English; an unknown format stays an ordinary message. A reaction you send also leaves as an SMS: a recent iPhone may show it as a reaction, other phones will simply show the text.

## Delete
- A message: right-click the bubble, then **Delete from Covalence**.
- A conversation: right-click it in the list, then **Delete conversation…**

!warn Deleting only affects Covalence: the message stays on the iPhone, which does not allow deleting messages over Bluetooth. It will not come back in Covalence, and new messages from the same person show as usual.

## Other conveniences
- Drafts kept per conversation, search, clickable links and numbers, canned replies.
- The number of unread messages shows on the Messages app icon.
