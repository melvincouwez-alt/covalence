The menu under the [Contacts](app:contacts) list offers two ways of working.

## Read over Bluetooth (default)
- Cards come from the iPhone (**Sync Contacts** must be on).
- They are read-only: search, photo, numbers, buttons to write or call.
- A copy stays on the computer so they show even when the iPhone is away.

## Manage with iCloud
- Create, edit and delete cards. iCloud then passes the changes on to the iPhone and your other devices.
- Your Apple Account must be connected: see [Apple Account](guide:apple).

!warn With iCloud, deleting a card also deletes it from the iPhone and all your devices. You are asked to confirm.
