## Install Covalence
1. Download the `.deb` package from the project's releases page.
2. Double-click it: it opens in Eddy (elementary OS) or in the App Center (Ubuntu).
3. Click **Install** and enter your password. The required packages are installed at the same time.
4. Open Covalence from the applications menu.

## Missing components
If something is missing on your system, Covalence says so in a **Missing components** card, at the start of the setup and in [Settings](app:settings).
1. Read the list: each line says what the component is for.
2. Click **Install**. You are asked for your password once.
3. Wait for the progress bar to finish. The card disappears when everything is in place.

!warn Some components cannot be installed from Covalence, for example a newer PipeWire for calls on elementary OS 8. The card then says "System update needed". The rest of Covalence still works.

## The setup assistant
On first launch, a full-window assistant welcomes you:
1. Choose what to connect: your iPhone, your Apple Account, or both.
2. Follow the steps. Each one ticks itself when it is done.
3. Choose which separate apps to show.
4. Finish. You can also skip the setup and come back later.

To run it again: [Settings › First-run setup](app:setup).
