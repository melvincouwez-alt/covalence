## Now Playing
The [Now Playing](app:nowplaying) page shows what the iPhone is playing: music, a podcast, an audiobook or a video, whatever the app.

- You see the title, the artist, the album, the app that plays and the progress.
- The buttons control the iPhone: previous, play or pause, next, and the iPhone's volume.
- **Play** works even when nothing is shown: the iPhone resumes what it last played.
- A mini player shows at the bottom of the sidebar, above **Settings**. Click it to open the page.
- The iPhone does not send the artwork. Covalence looks it up on Apple's public search service (iTunes) with the artist and the title only, and keeps it only when both match. Details in [Privacy](guide:privacy).

!tip The **iPhone music** row of the [Overview](app:device) turns this on or off. Without the Bluetooth Low Energy link, Covalence uses the classic Bluetooth remote control (AVRCP): the app name and the volume are then missing.

## iPhone sound
When the connected iPhone plays music or a video, the sound can come out of the computer.

- In the [Overview](app:device), the **iPhone sound** row turns this on or off.
- You can choose the output (speakers, headset) when the computer has several.
- Turning the iPhone sound off does not affect calls, which have their own setting (PC audio).
