## Before you start
- Bluetooth must be on, on both the computer and the iPhone.
- Keep the iPhone unlocked, within a metre of the computer.

## Pair
1. In the [Overview](app:device), click **Pair an iPhone…**
2. A six-digit code shows on the computer and on the iPhone. Check that they match.
3. Tap **Pair** on the iPhone, then confirm on the computer.
4. The Overview now shows your iPhone's name and battery.

!tip If the iPhone does not list the computer in Settings › Bluetooth, that is normal for this kind of accessory. Install the free **nRF Connect** app (Nordic Semiconductor) on the iPhone, start a scan, then tap **Connect** next to "Covalence". Pairing starts.

## Allow sharing
On the iPhone, open **Settings › Bluetooth**, then tap **ⓘ** next to your computer's name. Turn on:
- **Show Notifications**: gives access to messages.
- **Sync Contacts**: names, photos and call history.
- **Share System Notifications**: notifications from all your apps.

Depending on the iOS version, some switches only appear after a first connection: come back later if you do not see them.

## Notifications and music
These two features use a second, "low energy" link that only the iPhone can open. It usually comes up by itself after pairing. If notifications do not arrive, open nRF Connect on the iPhone and tap **Connect** next to "Covalence".

## Reconnecting
Covalence reconnects the iPhone when it comes back in range. If it does not, click **Reconnect** in the [Overview](app:device).
