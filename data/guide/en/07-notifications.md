!warn **Share System Notifications** must be on, on the iPhone, and the low energy link must be open. See [Connect your iPhone](guide:link).

## How it works
- The iPhone's notifications show like the computer's own.
- The [Notifications](app:notifications) page lists them, app by app. One switch per app chooses whether it also shows on the computer.
- When the iPhone offers them, a notification has its actions (accept, decline…).
- The list is only kept in memory, never on disk. **Clear list** changes nothing on the iPhone.

!tip When reading messages is not allowed, received messages also come this way: they show in Messages, marked "Received as a notification".
